Imagine staying in wild pristine environments of Bandhavgarh forest where the serene silence of jungle would only be broken by chirping of the birds.

Welcome to The Wildflower Resort — driven by a philanthropic goal of both protecting the environment and giving back to the local community.

Good to know

Questions before you arrive

Answers from The Wildflower Resort’s published visitor information.

The Tiger Reserve remains open for tourism from October 16th to June 30th. Winters suit verdant forest feeling; summers offer clearer views of forest and wildlife.

Deluxe Cottage Room: EP Rs. 2490/-, CP Rs. 2990/-, AP Rs. 4590/-. Suite Cottage Room: EP Rs. 4490/-, CP Rs. 4990/-, AP Rs. 5990/-. Confirm current rates when you enquire.

A jeep safari in Bandhavgarh involves booking through the mponline website (not the Madhya Pradesh Tourism website), hiring a jeep and engaging an official Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve guide. The resort can assist with safari booking on request.

More than 30 days prior to check-in: 100% refund. 30 to 14 days: 50% refund. 14 to 7 days: 25% refund. 7 to 0 days prior: no refund.

Nearest airports: Jabalpur (175 km) and Khajuraho (232 km). Nearest rail: Umaria (32 km), Katni (92 km), Satna (120 km). Road links from Umaria, Katni, Shahdol, Rewa and Jabalpur. Air-conditioned luxury sedans transfer guests from airports and stations.

Obtain a permit, take an official guide, keep speed under 20 km/h, no horns or litter, no mobile phones inside the park, don’t alight unless told, and remember — animals have the right of way. Shoot only with a camera.
